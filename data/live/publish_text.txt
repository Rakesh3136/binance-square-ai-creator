🚨 Standard Chartered launches spot Bitcoin and Ether trading in UAE

Source: Cointelegraph

📰 Standard Chartered just opened spot $BTC and $ETH trading for UAE institutions, becoming the first major global bank to run spot crypto directly on its institutional eFX rails, per Cointelegraph.

This shifts the setup. Instead of relying exclusively on crypto-native exchanges, regional funds can now settle spot digital assets on the same infrastructure used for traditional FX.

With $BTC holding firm above $81,000 following $731M in single-day US ETF inflows, institutional plumbing is broadening right as macro headwinds begin to clear.
