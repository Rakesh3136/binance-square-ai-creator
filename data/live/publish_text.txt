🚨 Standard Chartered launches spot Bitcoin and Ether trading in UAE

Source: Cointelegraph

📰 Standard Chartered has launched institutional spot $BTC and $ETH trading in the UAE, making it the first top global bank to offer spot crypto on its eFX rails in the region, according to Cointelegraph.

This is major infrastructure evolution.

Bringing digital assets onto the exact same trading rails institutions use for fiat FX removes operational friction for Middle Eastern institutional capital.

With Bitcoin holding above $81,000 after taking $731M in daily ETF inflows, structural banking integration is deepening right as macro liquidity opens up.
