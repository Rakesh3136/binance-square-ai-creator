# NIC Creator Discovery and High-Volume Plan

## Goal

Build a consistently useful, original crypto-trader creator. Volume is a scaling outcome, not a substitute for evidence or reader value. Revenue is explicitly out of scope for this plan.

## Workstream A — Discover trader problems

1. Collect 50–100 public trader questions, complaints, and unresolved discussions from multiple independent public sources.
2. Store canonical URL, source name, capture time, topic, trader segment, short paraphrase, evidence quality, and deduplication key.
3. Cluster repeated problems and rank by frequency, severity, current workaround, time sensitivity, and whether a small product/content test can address it.
4. Keep source facts separate from NIC interpretations. Do not infer private creator earnings or private analytics from public views.

## Workstream B — Study creator craft without copying

1. Sample at least 30 distinct creators across short-term setups, swing trading, market structure, risk education, macro/cross-asset analysis, and long-term project research.
2. Capture up to five public posts per creator when available. Record URL and timestamp, topic, reader promise, evidence citations, risk/invalidation language, format, chart relevance, originality, and follow-up/outcome evidence.
3. Learn reusable techniques (for example, explaining a mechanism, stating what would falsify a thesis, showing a counterargument). Never copy text, distinctive phrasing, or chart assets.
4. Compare NIC drafts against a generic baseline using blinded human review for accuracy, usefulness, clarity, originality, and risk disclosure.

## Workstream C — Validate before building

- Experiment 1: trader problem discovery — 50–100 discussions, shortlist five problems with multiple independent examples.
- Experiment 2: creator research — 30 creators, up to five posts each; report coverage gaps and transferable writing patterns.
- Experiment 3: pre-move market replay — timestamp-safe historical snapshots, non-winners included, fees/slippage and no look-ahead leakage.
- Experiment 4: blinded draft comparison — 10 matched pairs scored for accuracy, reader benefit, originality, clarity, risk disclosure, and chart correctness.

A failed or incomplete evidence collection run must be reported as such; it must not generate fabricated creator rankings or pretend a research sample was gathered.

## High-volume strategy: staged, quality-first

The volume policy module only recommends a cadence tier. It never calls the publisher and never changes a publishing feature flag.

- **Stage 0 — Draft only:** default until at least 20 drafts are reviewed, at least 90% pass the quality rubric, critical errors are zero, duplicate rate is at most 5%, chart-symbol match is at least 98%, source citation coverage is at least 95%, seven consecutive cycles are healthy, and there are no substantiated quality complaints.
- **Stage 1 — Eligible for one post/day:** after Stage 0 gates pass. Requires a separate, explicit publisher authorization and verified post IDs.
- **Stage 2 — Eligible for two posts/day:** only after seven verified pilot days, at least seven verified posts, at least 90% pilot quality pass rate, zero critical errors, and zero substantiated quality complaints.
- **Stage 3 — Eligible for up to three posts/day:** only after 14 verified pilot days, at least 20 verified pilot posts, at least 95% quality pass rate, and all earlier gates remain satisfied.

These are conservative internal targets, not Binance platform limits. Stop or step down immediately on a critical factual error, wrong chart/symbol, duplicate pattern, unsupported claim, or credible quality complaint. Do not publish merely to meet a quota. If no sufficiently good story exists, skip the slot.

## Editorial portfolio

Rotate among distinct reader benefits rather than repeating coin calls:
- market mechanism / BTC-to-alt relative-strength explanation;
- early candidate watchlist with clear disconfirming conditions;
- risk-management case study;
- long-term project evidence scorecard with primary sources;
- post-event review showing what the earlier thesis got right or wrong;
- trader problem explainer based on real public questions.

No category is entitled to publish if its evidence or visual validation fails. Do not label relative strength or open interest as proof of whale flows. Do not claim a long-term project will grow without primary-source evidence and a clear invalidation case.

## Required research record

For each creator post or trader-problem observation, preserve source URL, capture timestamp, topic/segment, evidence summary, confidence, and what NIC learned. For each experiment, preserve sample definition, baseline, evaluation rubric, result, failure cases, and next action. Treat missing data as missing, never as a positive result.

## Definition of done

- Research coverage is counted from actual source records, not hardcoded targets.
- Creator techniques are generalized and rewritten originally.
- Draft quality is evaluated against a baseline.
- Volume stage is derived from fresh, auditable metrics.
- Publisher authorization remains a separate control.
- No claim of successful self-learning without an evaluation result.
