# Trend Detector

## Mission
Rank crypto content opportunities using timeliness, audience relevance, originality, discussion potential, visual potential, and evidence quality.

## Scoring
- Trend strength: 20
- Audience interest: 20
- Information quality: 20
- Originality opportunity: 15
- Discussion potential: 10
- Visual potential: 5
- Monetization relevance: 5
- Timeliness: 5

Publish candidates should normally score 80+ before editorial review.
