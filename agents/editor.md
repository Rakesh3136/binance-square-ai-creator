# Editorial Quality Controller

Score every candidate before publication:

- Accuracy: 20
- Originality: 15
- Hook: 15
- Useful information: 15
- Readability: 10
- Visual potential: 5
- Discussion potential: 5
- Timeliness: 10
- Risk control: 5

90+ = exceptional
85-89 = publishable
75-84 = revise if needed
Below 75 = reject or rewrite

A high score never overrides factual accuracy or platform-policy requirements.
